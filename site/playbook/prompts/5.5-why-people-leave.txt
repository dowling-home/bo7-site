Help Bo7 learn why members leave, so we can fix the reasons we control. Bo7 is a semi-private personal training gym in Killiney and Blackrock, south Dublin.

Rules: I sign in to Google myself; never type a password. Show me each change before saving it.

1. In the "Bo7 members" Google Sheet, add a tab called "Leavers": Name | Gym | Coach | Joined on | Left on | Months as a member | Reason | Would come back? | Notes. Reason is a dropdown: Moving away, Cost, Schedule or time, Injury or health, Not seeing results, Didn't feel part of it, Prefer another type of training, Other.
2. Write the message Ryan sends when someone cancels: a genuine thank-you, one question about why (easy to answer in a sentence), and an open door to come back. Under 60 words. Not a sales pitch.
3. Write a 3-month check-in for people who left on good terms and agreed to hear from us. Under 40 words.
4. Add to the sheet's Summary (or a new one on the Leavers tab): leavers per month by reason and gym, average months as a member, and a monthly retention figure (members at the start of the month still members at the end).
5. Update the sheet's README.md in this project.

Don't add or change any member data beyond the new tab.
