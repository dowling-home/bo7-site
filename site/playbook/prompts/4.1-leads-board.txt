Build a leads board at team.bo7.ie/leads: one web page the Bo7 team opens every morning to see every lead in play, where each one came from, and a one-tap way to get back to them. It sits behind the Cloudflare login (only @bo7.ie email addresses), and reads from and writes to the "Bo7 leads" Google Sheet (its README.md describes the columns).

Rules: never put a password, key or token in the code. The Google service account key is already stored as an encrypted secret in the Cloudflare Pages project. Leads are personal data: nothing about them goes anywhere except this page and the sheet.

How it works: a small server-side function in the bo7-team project reads and updates the sheet using the service account, and only answers requests that came through the Cloudflare login. The page talks only to that function.

What the board shows:
1. A column per stage, left to right: New (no reply yet), Contacted, Consult booked, Showed up, Not yet (following up). Joined and Lost are hidden by default, with a toggle to show them.
2. Each lead is a card: name, gym, source and source detail, how long since they got in touch (or since the last contact), and the next step due. Cards waiting over 24 hours for a first reply are clearly marked at the top of New.
3. On each card, three buttons:
   - WhatsApp: opens WhatsApp to their number with the first-reply script for their source already typed (scripts are in the project; ask me for the file if you can't find it). Later stages use the matching follow-up or reminder message.
   - Call: dials their number.
   - Email: opens an email to them, if we have an address.
   Show the number and email as text too, in case a button doesn't work on someone's device.
4. Quick updates on each card: Contacted, Consult booked (with date and time), Showed up, Joined, Not yet (with reason and follow-up date), Lost. Each one writes back to the sheet, fills in First reply the first time a lead is marked Contacted, and notes the logged-in coach's email.
5. Filters at the top: gym, source, and "only mine".
6. A one-line summary at the top: waiting for a reply, follow-ups due today, consults in the next two days.

Make it work well on a phone, since most replies are sent from one. Use the Bo7 website's colours and fonts. Add the board to the team.bo7.ie home page.
