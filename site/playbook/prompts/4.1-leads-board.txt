Build a leads board at team.bo7.ie/leads: one web page the Bo7 team opens every morning to see every lead in play, get back to them in one tap, book them in for a free consultation, and keep notes. It sits behind the Cloudflare login (only @bo7.ie email addresses).

Where the data lives:
- The "Bo7 leads" Google Sheet (its README.md describes the tabs): the Leads tab for each lead, the Notes tab for notes, the Consult hours tab for when each coach takes consults.
- The shared "Bo7 consultations" Google Calendar for bookings. The calendar decides what is free, so two people can never book the same slot.

Rules: never put a password, key or token in the code. The Google service account key is already stored as an encrypted secret in the Cloudflare Pages project, and the service account can edit both the sheet and the calendar. Leads are personal data: nothing about them goes anywhere except this page, the sheet and the calendar.

How it works: a small server-side function in the bo7-team project reads and updates the sheet and the calendar using the service account, and only answers requests that came through the Cloudflare login. The page talks only to that function. Every change records the logged-in coach's email.

Design: match the dashboard mockup at site/dash/index.html in the bo7-site project (live at bo7.thedowlings.co/dash): the same layout, components, colours, light and dark themes, and the same lead dialog. Replace its sample data with the real sheet and calendar.

The board:
1. A summary strip: leads in this week by source, share answered within 24 hours, waiting over 24 hours for a first reply, and consults booked in the next 7 days (with the next one).
2. Where leads come from: count per source for the week or the last 4 weeks, with a gym filter.
3. Leads: everyone not booked yet, in two groups. "Not contacted yet" comes first, oldest first, with anyone over 24 hours marked: this is where the 24-hour rule applies. "Contacted" follows: people who have had a reply but haven't agreed a consult time, showing when they were last contacted and their latest note. A lead moves from the first group to the second when it's marked Contacted, and leaves the column when a consult is booked. Each card shows name, gym, source, preferred contact and how long they've waited, with WhatsApp, Call and Email buttons; the preferred one stands out. WhatsApp opens the chat with the first-reply script for their source already typed (scripts are in the project; ask me for the file if you can't find it). Show the number and email as text too, in case a button doesn't work.
4. Consults booked: booked consults only, by date and time, with the coach. Nothing else goes in this column.
5. Filters for gym, and "only mine" (leads the signed-in coach logged or is booked with).
5a. Parked leads: at the bottom of the Leads column, a short "Parked to try again" list (name, status, reason, try-again date). On their Follow up on date, Not now and Couldn't reach leads come back into the Contacted group marked "Try again", but only if Consent to contact is Yes. Lost leads never come back.

The lead dialog (opens when someone clicks a lead):
6. The lead's details, contact buttons, and quick status updates: Contacted, Showed up, Joined, Not yet (with reason and follow-up date). Marking Contacted the first time fills in First reply.
   Remove from board, for leads not booked: pick why (Couldn't reach them, Not interested right now, Don't contact again) and, unless it's Don't contact again, when to try again (1, 3 or 6 months). It sets Status to Couldn't reach, Not now or Lost, sets Follow up on, adds a note with the reason (using the note box if something is typed), and takes the lead off the board. Nothing is deleted.
7. Book a consultation:
   - Pick the gym (defaults to the lead's), a coach or "any coach", and a day in the next 14 days.
   - Free times = the Consult hours tab for that coach, gym and day, in slots of the consult length, minus anything already on the Bo7 consultations calendar for that coach. Hide times in the past.
   - Booking creates an event on the Bo7 consultations calendar: title "Consult: [lead name], [gym], with [coach]", the gym's address as the location, and the lead's phone, source and a link back to the board in the description. Don't add the lead or coach as invited guests.
   - It then writes to the sheet: Status "Consult booked", Consult (date and time), Consult coach, Calendar event. And it adds a note: "Consult booked for [day, time] with [coach]".
   - Once booked, show a button to send the confirmation on WhatsApp, with the booking confirmation message (prompt 4.2) already typed, including day, time, gym, address and coach.
   - For a booked lead, show the booking and let the coach move it (update the same event) or cancel it (delete the event, clear the consult fields, add a note).
8. Notes: the lead's notes from the Notes tab, newest first, with who wrote each and when, and a box to add one. Adding a note writes a new row to the Notes tab.
